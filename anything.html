◆ § 03 — Function · what it can do

Six things the lattice does for you.

Capabilities · v01
On-wrist · sub-second
01 — Reform

Becomes any tool on demand.

Plates separate, swirl, and re-bond into a blade, a thruster, a curved shield — any shape the lattice has been taught. Average reform time, surface to silhouette: 0.34 seconds.

↗
02 — Self-repair

Closes its own wounds.

Damaged cells signal their neighbours. The lattice redistributes mass into the breach, reseals the surface, and continues operating without dropping a frame. Up to 9 cm² per second.

↗
03 — Modular

Coverage to fit the moment.

Worn as a wristband, it spreads on cue: bracer, gauntlet, half-sleeve, full plate. Mass stays constant — the cells just choose where to be. No stowage, no swap-outs, no hand-off.

↗
04 — Intent-read

Reads the nerve, not the button.

A skin-side neural cuff samples motor intent at 1.6 kHz. The lattice anticipates a posture before the muscle finishes the move — so the tool is in your hand at the bottom of the gesture.

↗
05 — Kinetic dampening

Absorbs the hit, returns the heat.

Incoming force is dispersed across the lattice and routed into a thermal sink. Energy that would have bruised the wrist comes back out as a controlled pulse — or stored against the next reform.

↗
06 — Camouflage

Dresses for the room.

Surface micro-cells re-pigment in 80 ms. The same lattice that becomes a shield can settle back into a matte black bracelet under a sleeve. Loud when it has to be, quiet by default.
