◆ § 02 — Features

Restless precision.

Every component earns its place. Six things we obsess over so you don't have to.

  • 01 — Movement

    Sub-millisecond sensing.

    9-axis IMU sampled at 1.6 kHz. Catches a wrist tremor before you do.

  • 02 — Material

    Grade-5 titanium, end to end.

    CNC-machined in a single pass. Sapphire crystal, no plastics anywhere on the body.

  • 03 — Endurance

    14-day ambient battery.

    Solid-state cell. 36 hours under continuous workout telemetry. Wireless charge in 25 minutes.

  • 04 — Navigation

    Dual-band L1/L5 GPS.

    Locks in 1.8 seconds. Holds position inside subway tunnels, parking decks, and Manhattan canyons.

  • 05 — Agentic OS

    Anticipates your day.

    An on-device agent reads context, surfaces what matters, and silences what doesn't. No cloud dependency.

  • 06 — Water

    200m WR — actually.

    ISO 22810 certified. Swim, dive, and shower without the mental overhead.
